❓ Help

Welcome to the plushie builder app where you can build and receive the pattern for any plushie you want! Click the Edit Plushies button on the homepage to start your plushie building journey. Add and delete body parts and change their colors using the panel on the right, and create and delete plushies using the panel on the left. To see the pattern for your plushies, click on the Patterns button on the homescreen. Let your creativity flow and your crochet plushie building dreams come true!
